The goal

Every bill ends in credit.

Your Powerwall already stores power. wattle makes it trade, so the battery earns more than your home costs to run.

Clever, and careful.

Never exports at a loss.

When the feed-in price drops below the level you set, wattle stops sending solar to the grid and fills your battery instead.

Questions

Do I need to be with Amber?

For now, yes. wattle works by following Amber's live wholesale prices. If you're with someone else, register anyway and tell us who. We'll let you know whether switching makes sense for you.

Does wattle get my Tesla password?

No. You sign in on Tesla's own website and Tesla gives wattle permission for your energy products only. You can take it away any time in your Tesla account.

What if wattle goes offline?

Your Powerwall goes back to Self-Powered with your backup reserve, just as it was before wattle.

What does it cost?

Nothing. wattle is free for friends and family while it's new.1
