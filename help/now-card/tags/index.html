What the tags on the Now card mean

On Home, the Now card's headline says what your battery is doing. The tag beside it says who's running the battery: you, wattle, or the battery itself.

HoldingWatchingHow it looks on Home

Watching

wattle reads your battery, solar and prices every minute and works out a plan, but sends the battery nothing. The headline is what wattle would do.

Every home starts here. Your battery keeps running on its own settings, so you can see how wattle would do before you let it.

What to do: Nothing. When you're ready for wattle to take over, write to us.

In control

wattle is running your battery by its plan. The headline is what the battery is doing right now.

wattle never goes below your blackout reserve, and stops selling to the grid when the price you'd get goes negative.

What to do: Nothing. Tap the battery or grid buttons to see how today is going.

Manual

Someone in your home has set the battery from Controls for a while, for example to hold charge before a storm.

wattle follows that setting until it ends, then goes back to its plan by itself. Your blackout reserve still applies.

What to do: Nothing, unless you want to end it early from Controls.

Safe mode

wattle hasn't had a good reading from your battery, or prices, for 15 minutes. So the battery is back on Self-Powered, its own everyday setting.

This is on purpose. wattle would rather hand back than guess. It takes over again by itself once readings return.

What to do: Usually nothing. If it lasts more than an hour, check your internet and the Tesla app, then write to us.

Always true, whatever the tag

Your blackout reserve is the floor: wattle never takes the battery below it. Other people's homes you can see in wattle are watch only.

Still not sure? Write to wattle@nurthen.au and tell us what your Now card says.
