What "Keeping for tonight" means

The green line on the Now card, like "Keeping 7.0 kWh (26%) for tonight", is how much of your battery wattle won't sell, so your home runs on it until the sun is back.

Keeping 7.0 kWh (26%) for tonightHow it looks on Home

How wattle works it out

Every minute, wattle adds up what your home is likely to use from now until your solar covers the house again in the morning.

It counts from right now, not from bedtime, so the rest of the evening is included. And it counts until the solar really takes over, which can be later than you'd think on a cloudy morning.

As the night goes on there's less of it left, so the number goes down.

What a "big night" is

wattle doesn't plan for an average night. It plans for one of your heavier ones.

It looks back over your past nights and lines them up from lightest to heaviest. Then it keeps enough for a night near the heavy end: only about 1 night in 10 used more. That covers things like the heater, a late dishwasher or guests.

So on most nights you'll wake up with some battery left. That's not wasted: it's yours to use in the morning, and wattle can sell it if the morning price is good.

Until wattle has a few weeks of your own nights, it uses a careful estimate instead.

When prices are high

How much wattle keeps depends on what it could get for selling.

Normal prices: keep enough for a big night.
High prices: keep enough for a usual night, and sell a bit more.
Price spikes: keep only your backup reserve, and sell the rest while it's worth the most.

You can change these in Selling limits, and add an extra buffer on top if you'd like more kept.

Your backup reserve

The backup reserve you set in your battery's app is kept for blackouts, and it isn't part of this number.

wattle never uses your backup reserve. It stays for blackouts unless you lower it yourself, in your battery's app or in wattle. While wattle is watching, it reads the reserve from your battery's app every minute.

Does the number look wrong for your home? Write to wattle@nurthen.au and tell us what your Now card says.
